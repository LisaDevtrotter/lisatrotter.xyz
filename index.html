01 — About

A little about me.

I'm Lisa, from France. My work revolves around people, emerging technologies, and connecting talent with opportunities in ambitious teams.

I came to this through building on the web and reading the fine print — I still read the contracts and the design docs, and I tend to have one more question. In crypto and Web3 that curiosity matters: the interesting part isn't the price, it's who changes their behaviour once real work, and real money, are on the line.

I spend my time around builders, researchers and specialists across the ecosystem, and I like making the introductions that turn a conversation into something real.
